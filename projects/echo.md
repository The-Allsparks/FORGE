# ECHO

**Environmental Cue and Heading Output**

ECHO turns selected robot information into sound for the driver. It never commands robot hardware. Teach auditory cue design and driver workload. Begin in simulation or off-robot exercises. Require an immediate mute/disable path. Do not competition-enable without evidence that cues help the driver.

Authoritative repository: [The-Allsparks/ECHO](https://github.com/The-Allsparks/ECHO)

## What students learn here

A cue is a chosen message. Silence is valid. Center pan on unknown is a lie. Two-ear audio can hide referees.

## Prerequisites

Desktop tests. Hearing-safety briefing before any audible output ([hearing-safety.md](https://github.com/The-Allsparks/ECHO/blob/main/docs/hearing-safety.md)).

## Hardware / simulation

[phase1-desktop example](https://github.com/The-Allsparks/ECHO/blob/main/examples/phase1-desktop.md). Driver Hub path is **not** in the core library.

## Evidence

Cue decision records; mute-path demonstration; later, a written experiment — anecdotes are not approval.

## Safety

Start OS volume low and ECHO gain at default. Stop if ears ring or speech is hard. Mute must not stop the robot.
