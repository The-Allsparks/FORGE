### Priority hazards (prompt list)

Record when relevant — not every meeting:

- Capstan slip or wrap
- Belt tracking / derailment
- Tensile-core joint failure
- Elevator limit / overrun
- Jam behavior under load
- Electrical loading / brownout (observe with AMPER; AMPER does not fix bad wiring)
- Communication failures (BEACON observes; BEACON does not command recovery in early phases)
- Mechanism interlocks (MIMIC owns safe operation; hard stops before software limits)

HELM may request stop or intent but does not replace MIMIC safety or mechanical hard stops.
