# Evidence record

Fill this at the end of every substantial engineering session. About 5–10 minutes. If time is short, fill at least Date, Question, Observation, Decision, and Next test.

Do not include student full names, passwords, Wi-Fi keys, or raw camera video. Use first names and last initials only.
