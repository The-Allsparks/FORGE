# Student ownership and Initial Interview practice

Four students, fifth through seventh grade. Show authentic ownership without lowering technical ambition. **No scripted judge answers** — students explain what they built and tested.

## Subsystem relationship sketch

Draw or label how these connect on **your** robot:

- Pedro Pathing → chassis motion
- MIMIC → mechanism states, limits, interlocks
- HELM → intent only (no chassis authority; does not bypass MIMIC)
- TRACE → records what happened
- AMPER → electrical observation
- ViDAR → vision / field state
- BEACON → comms health
- ECHO → audio cues for the driver (not telemetry)

## Initial Interview practice (~10 minutes)

BIOBUZZ V0 uses an **Initial Interview** (not "Structured Interview"):

| Block | Time | What happens |
| ----- | ---- | ------------ |
| Judge intro | ~1 min | Judges introduce themselves |
| Student presentation | up to ~5 min | Optional prepared talk; may end early |
| Q&A | remainder | Judges ask questions; at least 10 min total |
| Minimum students | 2 | More is encouraged (A204) |

### Practice agenda (Meeting B closeout or S018)

1. Pick one engineering story from a [portfolio candidate](portfolio-candidate.md) or [judging one-pager](judging-one-pager.md).
2. Owner presents up to 3 minutes — point at robot, photo, or TRACE figure.
3. Teammate asks two questions the owner must answer without reading.
4. Mentor asks one "show me" question (not "tell me the award criteria").
5. If the answer needs seven library names, shorten it.

### Rules

- Do not memorize award definitions.
- Do not let the adult answer during practice (mirrors A208 silent observer rule at events).
- Recording real judge interviews is forbidden (A210). Practice recordings, if any, stay off git.
