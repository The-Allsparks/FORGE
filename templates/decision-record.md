### Prompt examples (students decide — FORGE does not)

Use these as questions, not as predetermined answers:

- Conventional winch versus capstan
- One long belt versus separate belt arrangements
- Surgical tubing versus urethane or other round belting
- Paracord versus Dyneema/HMPE tensile core
- Pulley diameters and groove profiles
- Mechanical versus software-based holding strategies
- Conventional control versus feedback-assisted control

If software is in the comparison: MIMIC owns safe mechanism operation; HELM may request intent but does not bypass MIMIC; Pedro Pathing owns chassis motion; TRACE records tests; AMPER supplies the power envelope.
