## What came from Pratt vs Allsparks adaptations

| Element | Pratt (original) | Allsparks adaptation |
| ------- | ---------------- | -------------------- |
| Timeline | ~12 weeks post-Kickoff to first competition | ~6–7 post-Kickoff weeks to League 1S/2S (31 Oct 2026, planning input) |
| Team size | Assumes larger veteran-style teams | Four-student rookie team (ages ~10–12) |
| Meeting cadence | Not specified for this team | Two meetings/week × ~2 hours (Mon/Fri; Wed preseason exceptions) |
| Ideation volume | Hundreds of visual concepts | 60–100 concepts across four students |
| Module count | ~4 mechanisms (DECODE-era example) | Derived from BIOBUZZ after Kickoff — not fixed at four |
| Two chassis | Separate programming and prototype robots | Hybrid Strafer software platform + season starter-bot fallback + bench fixtures — not two full competition copies |
| Software stack | General programming progression | ViDAR, AMPER, MIMIC, BEACON, TRACE, HELM, ECHO with minimum viable integration and enablement gates ([FORGE#4](https://github.com/The-Allsparks/FORGE/issues/4)) |
| Chassis motion | Not Pedro-specific | Pedro Pathing owns chassis motion; HELM does not take authority |
| Preseason | Week 1 starts at Kickoff | Extended preseason (P001–P008) before 12 Sep 2026 Kickoff |
| Post-first-competition | Week 12 focus | FORGE retains league-development, adversity, feature-freeze, and state-prep phases through Feb 2027 |
| Award/portfolio specifics | DECODE-era examples in videos | Use V0 tables in [award-and-portfolio-traceability.md](../../../docs/award-and-portfolio-traceability.md) (15 content pages); re-verify Kickoff. Strategy: [award-strategy.md](award-strategy.md). Videos are not the rule source. |
