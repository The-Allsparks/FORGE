# Preseason → Kickoff mechanism gate

Preseason experiments (capstan, tower slide, ball transport) were **deferred after Kickoff** when P007 was rescoped to drivetrain dial-in and Kickoff preparation. They still produce **reusable evidence**, not a speculative BIOBUZZ robot, and now belong in S001/S003 (or later) **only if** official BIOBUZZ requirements justify those principles. Do **not** place them on the P008 FRC tour. See [preseason-deferred-work.md](preseason-deferred-work.md).

The full tower/hopper/game-piece system does **not** proceed until official BIOBUZZ requirements justify it.

## Evaluate after Kickoff (official materials only)

- Game-piece dimensions, mass, and surface
- Scoring height and reach
- Required collection orientation
- Storage value and cycle-time value
- Starting configuration and expansion limits
- Field obstacles
- Autonomous opportunities
- Endgame requirements
- Serviceability and failure consequences

Use [kickoff-replan-guide.md](../kickoff-replan-guide.md), the [kickoff-decision-package.md](../../../templates/kickoff-decision-package.md), and [decision-record.md](../../../templates/decision-record.md). Tower/capstan/transport belong on the **not-yet** list unless the ranked capability table keeps them.

## Possible outcomes

- Continue the complete tower and transport concept
- Keep the capstan but redesign the tower
- Keep the transport but eliminate storage
- Use a shorter lift or horizontal extension
- Abandon the concept and reuse its components on a different MVP

Prototype parts must remain **reusable** if the team pivots. Do not permanently consume Strafer drivetrain parts on throwaway preseason-only builds.
