## Driving question

What can we **observe and ask** on an FRC team’s shop tour that will help us be better FTC students tomorrow — without copying FRC scale, rules, or hardware blindly?

## Student-facing objective

**Primary objective:** Complete a respectful FRC team tour with assigned observations, student questions, notes, and a thank-you follow-up.

**Student learning outcomes:**

- Explain the tour purpose in one sentence: learn how another robotics team works
- Use assigned observation jobs instead of clustering as a silent group
- Ask prepared questions in mechanical, electrical, programming, drive team, pit, safety, documentation, outreach, and team organization
- Record what they saw without claiming FORGE already knows the host’s name or layout
- Check any inspiring FRC practice against **FTC** scale, rules, hardware, budget, and four-student capacity
- Leave a thank-you / follow-up action
- Write a short post-tour reflection
- Remember that Kickoff worksheets were packed at P007 — tonight does not replace Saturday analysis

## Integrated build or test activity

There is no Allsparks robot test. The “integrated” product is **notes that distinguish observed facts from ideas to try in FTC**.

## Failure-injection scenario

Mentor (or a student) says “FRC teams use X, so we must do X this season.” Correct answer: write it as an **idea**, then check FTC rules, scale, hardware, budget, and capacity after Kickoff. Do not lock it tonight.

## Student questions (use as a bank; do not invent host answers)

**Mechanical**

- How do you decide a mechanism is good enough to keep?
- What breaks most often, and how do you make it easier to repair?

**Electrical**

- How do you label batteries and wires so pit repair is fast?
- What wiring mistake do new students make?

**Programming**

- How do you test code before it goes on the competition robot?
- Who is allowed to deploy, and how do you roll back?

**Drive team**

- How do drivers practice when the robot is not ready?
- What callouts do you use when something is unsafe?

**Pit operations**

- What is on your cart that you would not leave behind?
- How do you inspect the robot the same way every time?

**Safety**

- What shop rule is non-negotiable?
- How do you handle batteries?

**Documentation**

- What do you write down on the same day you build?
- How do you keep photos useful for judging later?

**Outreach and team organization**

- How do four (or more) people avoid silos?
- How do you thank people who help the team?

## Student explain-back questions

1. What was the purpose of today’s tour?
2. Name one thing you observed (fact) and one idea to check against FTC rules later.
3. Why might an FRC practice be too big, too expensive, or illegal for FTC?
4. Who owns the thank-you follow-up?
5. What meeting is tomorrow, and which documents count as rules?
