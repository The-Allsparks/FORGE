## Driving question

Can we deliver gratitude to sponsors **and** leave with a square, rigid chassis frame ready for drivetrain work?

## Student-facing objective

Students will finish, sign, and prepare first-wave sponsor thank-you cards; pull organized Strafer StarterBot chassis parts; assemble and square the main frame; **enter key frame dimensions into Onshape**; check fastener engagement, rigidity, and dimensions; and capture build evidence.

## Robot outcome

- First-wave sponsor cards ready for delivery or mailing
- Main chassis frame substantially assembled, squared, and photographed
- Dimensional and rigidity notes recorded for P003 drivetrain layout
- Onshape chassis sketch updated with measured rail spacing (or P001 Onshape catch-up completed first)

## Prerequisites

- P001 parts organization complete (or equivalent inventory)
- Sponsor card drafts from P001 or fresh start today
- goBILDA Strafer StarterBot chassis hardware staged
- Mentors present for tool use and square-check coaching

## Vocabulary

square · rigidity · fastener engagement · stewardship · chassis rail · build evidence

## Safety concerns

- Pinch points during frame clamping and tightening
- Allen keys and wrenches — no horseplay
- Safety glasses required in build area
- No motors or battery connected today unless mentor schedules an exception (default: **no power**)

## Required hardware

- Strafer StarterBot chassis kit parts (from P001 organizers)
- Square, measuring tape or calipers, level surface
- Hand tools per kit instructions
- Card materials, pens, envelopes, stamps (mentor-held)

## Required software

- Camera for progress photos

## Preparation required before the meeting

## Mentor demonstration

Three minutes: demonstrate square check on one corner (measure diagonals or use a square tool). Students repeat on their frame before the inspection + CAD block.

| Path | Work |

## Integrated build or test activity

Frame assembly and square check **is** the product. Cards and chassis both must show progress — not one at the expense of the other.

## Failure-injection scenario

Mentor loosens one corner brace without announcing which. Square-check pair must find and fix it before closeout.

## Evidence to collect

- Sponsor cards ready for delivery or mailing (photo — redact addresses)
- Chassis frame progress photographs (multiple angles)
- Onshape chassis sketch with measured dimensions
- Student explain-back on fastener engagement and next build step

## Student explain-back questions

1. How did you verify the frame was square?
2. What is still missing before drivetrain motors can mount?
3. Why is sponsor thank-you work **stewardship** evidence?
4. Where are oversized parts stored (from P001)?

## Assessment or exit check

Frame is substantially assembled with documented square check; cards are ready for mentor to mail; photos captured.

## Portfolio or engineering-notebook artifact

Before/after frame photo. Sponsor stewardship paragraph (Sustain candidate). Design notebook entry: frame dimensions and square method (Design/Innovate candidate).

## Competition enablement impact

No powered features. Mechanical build evidence only.

## Rollback procedure

Disassemble only the joint that failed inspection — do not strip the whole frame unless unsafe.

## Cleanup requirements

Tools returned; loose fasteners bagged; frame on stable surface; card materials secured.
