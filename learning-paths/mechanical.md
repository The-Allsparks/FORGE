# Mechanical learning path

Mechanical work is the default Meeting A occupation. Software layers describe the mechanism you are building; they do not replace it.

## Foundation

- Hand tools, fasteners, structure square enough to drive
- Drivetrain assembly and wheel retention
- Cable routing channels and strain relief (work with electrical)
- Hard stops and pinch points before any MIMIC limit discussion

## Developing

- Mechanism of the week for BIOBUZZ (after Kickoff)
- Repeatable mounting for one camera (ViDAR) without blocking intake
- Serviceability: battery swap, module access, inspection

## Integration

- Mechanism geometry that can be named as MIMIC states
- Keep mass and wiring compatible with AMPER observations (loose XT30 is a mechanical/electrical defect, not an AMPER bug)

## Competition readiness

- Inspection-legal fasteners and guards
- Pit rebuild of one failed subsystem in a timed drill

## Pairing

Always pair with electrical or programming for the last 25 minutes of Meeting A so the thing you built is observed (TRACE/MIMIC snapshot) the same day.

Authoritative mechanism safety: [MIMIC safety model](https://github.com/The-Allsparks/MIMIC/blob/main/docs/mechanism-control/safety-model.md).
