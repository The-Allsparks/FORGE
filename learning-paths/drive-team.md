# Drive-team learning path

Driver practice is a first-class deliverable, not leftover time.

## Foundation

- Safe enable, disable, e-stop awareness
- Baseline teleop on blocks, then carpet
- Callouts: "stop", "battery", "can't see"

## Developing

- Timed cycles on the practice surface
- Conventional auto start procedure
- Reading TRACE/DS after a run (one thing that surprised you)

## Integration

- Clinic: workload notes while optional systems are **passive or off**
- ECHO only in controlled drills with mute; competition remains off until evidence
- Human factors: do not drown referee audio ([ECHO hearing safety](https://github.com/The-Allsparks/ECHO/blob/main/docs/hearing-safety.md))

## Competition readiness

- Match checklist, alliance communication, timeout/rollback language
- Feature freeze: no new active cues or planners

## Pairing

Drivers still answer explain-backs. A driver who cannot say what happens if vision is stale is not ready for ViDAR-influenced driving (and ViDAR should not influence driving yet).
